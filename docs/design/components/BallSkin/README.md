# BallSkin

The ball's own surface (a texture or a built-in Material on the sphere) and the upright topper that floats above it. A set is one of each sold together.

- **Consumer provides**: the skin id, its texture asset id (in `Assets.luau`) or Material + `w-*` colour, an optional topper id, and how it unlocks (coins, tier reward, book, Robux).
- **Use it** in the shop, the hub and the results screen, where the ball is not yet covered.
- **Do** put every face, ear or eye on the topper: a face on the skin spins upside down as the ball rolls. Design skins for the first 30 seconds: big shapes, 2–3 colours, readable at 2 studs.
- **Do** keep sports balls generic: no league, maker or tournament logos or official colourways.
- **Don't** copy a meme's image, name or music (see *Player cosmetics and shop*), use transparency, rarity colours or "limited" labels. Golden is never sold.
