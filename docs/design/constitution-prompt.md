```text
Amend the Rollossal constitution (.specify/memory/constitution.md, now 1.1.0) for the design system in docs/design/ (README.md, 01-world-kit.md, 05-roblox-limits.md, 06-art-style.md). The owner approved these changes on 2026-10-28. Keep every other principle, the Quality Gates, Human Approval and Governance as they are, and keep the document's plain style.

1. Principle XI (Asset Pipeline): replace "Assets MUST be low-poly and flat-shaded, MUST share one palette texture" with the stylised-real rules:
   - World assets MUST be low-poly in real-world proportions. Silhouette edges get one bevel segment. Round parts MAY be smooth-shaded and hard edges stay sharp.
   - Every world mesh MUST use the theme's gradient atlas: one 1,024 px texture per theme, all with the same layout, generated by script from docs/design/tokens.json. Each face's UVs MUST sit inside one atlas cell or one trim strip, and each mesh has one material.
   - A theme swaps the atlas and never the meshes.
   - World assets MUST NOT use SurfaceAppearance/PBR maps, transparency or a light per prop. Glow is Material.Neon.
   - Ball cosmetics (skins) MAY use their own texture of at most 512 px. Toppers use the world atlas.
   - Keep the triangle budgets in src/shared/Config, Blender as the only tool, art/source and art/export, Assets.luau, and the Creator Store rule unchanged.
   - Update the rationale: one atlas per theme keeps phones fast and makes a season a texture swap. Stylised real reads as a real city for the 16+ launch audience, where flat-shaded toy art reads as a kids' game.

2. Principle VII (Content Is Data):
   - Replace the reskin example "Winter to Candy" with "Holiday City to Sunny City".
   - Add: a world's theme MUST be data. A theme is a palette atlas, lighting, weather, music, dressing slots, overlays, hero variants and a book page. The active theme is chosen by id in data. Switching it is a content change shipped in a release, never a code change and never a date check shown to players.

3. Principle IV (Safe for Everyone):
   - Add: the ball MUST NOT absorb people or real or live animals. Stand-ins such as statues, inflatables or decorations are fine. Ambient animals stay non-absorbable.
   - Add: no real brands, logos, flags, meme images or real people's faces, and no words on textures (pictograms only).
   - Update the rationale: the launch audience is age-checked 16+ players during the trial, then 10–15. Every asset stays Minimal-safe, so the same build works for both groups without a reskin.

4. The preamble says the design spec is docs/rollossal-gdd.md. Add that the art and content direction is docs/design/ and that it is subordinate to this constitution.

Bump the version as Governance requires: XI's "flat-shaded" rule is redefined, so judge whether that is MAJOR or MINOR and say why. Set Last Amended to 2026-10-28. Write the Sync Impact Report. Update dependent templates and agent files only where they repeat the changed rules. Do not edit docs/rollossal-gdd.md or docs/design/. Do not commit or push.
```
