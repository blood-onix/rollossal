# Launch theme: Holiday City

Downtown launches in December in the `winter` theme, **Holiday City**. It is the same map as every other season (same layout, gates, shortcuts and pacing), dressed as a city on the last evening before the holidays. In January the active theme switches to `city` (Sunny City) by data. Holiday City replaces the Snow Village content as the launch world.

## The feel

Golden hour on a snowy city street: warm lit windows, garlands over the road, a market in the Plaza, a huge tree where the duck usually floats. Festive, not cute: no elves, no plush, no cartoon Santa. The joke stays the same: you roll up the whole holiday.

## Theme layers

| Layer | Holiday City |
| --- | --- |
| Palette | The `winter` tokens: white `w-season` caps on every top face, white roofs, snowy pavements, amber `w-glass` windows, holiday red and gold accents. |
| Lighting | `clockTime` about 16.5, warm sun, `light-fog` and `light-sky` from the tokens, fog from about 150 studs. |
| Weather | Snowfall emitter on the camera, at most 40 particles, off with Reduce motion. |
| Music | A looping holiday track (`Assets.music`, still empty: owner picks one). |
| Slots | The `winter` column in *World kit and seasons*, Pattern 2. |
| Overlays | The Christmas market and street dressing below. |
| Hero variants | Below. |
| Book page | *Downtown: Holiday*, all theme-only objects. It returns every December. |

## Store page and launch

- Icon and thumbnails show Holiday City: a giant snowball-like ball with gifts stuck in it, mid-roll on Market Street. The title stays seasonless ("Rollossal: Roll Up Everything").
- Holiday cosmetics (an Ornament skin, a Santa-hat topper, a Sleigh Tracks trail) belong to the shop feature, return every December and are never "limited".

## January switch

- Set the active theme to `city`. Overlays, hero variants and holiday slots leave with the theme; the base map is untouched.
- The *Downtown: Holiday* book page stays visible with progress kept, marked as returning next December.
- If Sunny City dressing is not ready, `city` runs on palette and lighting only (Pattern 1) until it is.
