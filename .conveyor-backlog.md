## spec: 001: Grey-box prototype: ball control, absorb, growth, camera
<!-- id: daa78c3e -->

Build the first playable grey-box of Rollossal: one rolling ball per player that sticks to anything small enough, grows by volume, and has a camera that pulls back as it grows. One room with 30 cubes in 4 sizes. The goal is a fun test with 6 people, and proof that the core physics (a growing sphere with welded parts) works without jitter. Design brief: README.md §3.1, §3.2, §12 (risks). Constitution: I, II, III, VI, X, XII.

This is the gate feature: under constitution principle I, no art, monetization or live-ops work may start until this build has been playtested and the owner has signed off.

### Part B: pure rules (src/shared/Rules, Lune-tested with fixed expected values)
- Absorb rule: absorbable if `size ≤ absorbRatio × ballDiameter` (absorbRatio 0.4, proposed). Test the boundary (equal passes, just above fails).
- Growth by volume: `newD = (D³ + k · size³)^(1/3)`, k = 0.6 (proposed). Also the inverse, for knock-offs: removing stuck objects shrinks the ball by their volume, never below the start diameter.
- Speed: grows with diameter^0.5, capped. Camera distance: 4 × diameter, clamped.
- Expected values in tests are hand-computed, never produced by the code under test.

### Part C: config (src/shared/Config)
All tuning numbers and budgets live here, not in scripts: startDiameter 2, absorbRatio 0.4, growthK 0.6, maxVisibleStuck 30, knockOffCount 3, bumpSpeedThreshold, absorbRateLimit 10/s, absorbDistanceTolerance, speed exponent and cap, max server-accepted ball speed, camera multiplier and clamp, jump impulse. Also a feature-flag table (constitution VII) and the budgets from principle VI (visible stuck objects per ball, active parts per server, triangles per mesh).

### Part D: server
- BallService: spawns a `Part` with `Shape = Ball` per player, gives the client network ownership, and sanity-checks speed every heartbeat against Config (correct or reset out-of-range movement; log the violation).
- AbsorbService: one RemoteEvent that accepts only an object ID. Type-check the argument, then validate: the object exists and is not taken; distance ≤ ballRadius + size/2 + tolerance; the size rule passes; the player is under the rate limit. On success the server applies growth, marks the object taken, and publishes the authoritative diameter (for example as an attribute). The client never sends sizes, scores or diameters.
- Bumping: an object above absorb size is a solid obstacle. A hit faster than the bump threshold knocks off the 3 most recent stuck objects, which scatter and can be re-collected, and the ball shrinks by their volume. The server decides; the plan chooses how hits are detected (server-side touch plus relative speed, or a client report the server verifies).
- Simple respawn so the 30-cube room does not run dry during a 6-person test (the "out of sight" rule and pooling come in feature 004).

### Part E: client
- BallController: input from `Humanoid.MoveDirection` (thumbstick or WASD), applied as torque through an `AngularVelocity` constraint plus a small forward force. The avatar is hidden and the player *is* the ball; if the character is removed, read the move vector from the PlayerModule ControlModule instead (plan decides).
- Jump: a small hop for kerbs and stairs. A large on-screen button on mobile (at least 96 px), Space on PC.
- CameraController: follows the ball; distance follows the diameter with smoothing.
- StuckVisuals: welds the object at the contact point with `CanCollide = false` and `Massless = true`. Only the 30 most recent stay visible; older ones count as buried and are removed.

### Part F: grey-box room
One room with 30 cubes in 4 sizes. The plan picks sizes and counts from the growth formula so each size becomes absorbable in turn within a couple of minutes, and shows the maths. If 30 cubes cannot complete the chain, use respawns or more cubes and record why. Plain parts, no art.

### Risks to prove in this feature
- Jitter from resizing a rolling sphere with welded parts (README §12). Measure it. If welding jitters, switch to the fallback: stuck objects are client-side visuals that follow the ball and are not welded. Record the result in the plan.
- Speed exploits from client-owned physics: covered by the server speed check.

### Tests
- tests/unit (Lune): absorb rule, growth and shrink maths, speed and camera curves, Config sanity (numbers positive, caps above minimums).
- tests/studio (Jest-Lua): absorb validation rejects a missing object, an already-taken object, an out-of-range distance, a too-large object, a non-string or unknown ID, and the 11th request in one second.

### Playtest (constitution I)
`specs/<feature>/playtest.md`: date, commit, participant counts per age group (at least 3 adults and 3 children aged 10–12; children by age group only, never by name), findings and the owner's sign-off. Measure: first object rolled up within 5 seconds, jitter, does growth feel satisfying, frame rate on a phone.

### Acceptance
- scripts/check.sh exits 0 locally and in CI.
- Up to 12 players can each roll their own ball in one server; 6 have played together.
- Every absorb goes through server validation; nothing about size comes from the client.
- A knock-off scatters 3 objects that can be re-collected, and the ball shrinks.
- No new console errors or warnings in a Studio playtest driven through the Studio MCP server.

### Out of scope
Run timer, stars and results (002); saving (003); art, audio, VFX and UI styling (004 and later); merging (005).

## spec: 002: Run loop: timer, tiers, stars, results, Roll again
<!-- id: 902159ef -->

Turn free rolling into 4-minute runs with a clear ending: a personal timer, size tiers that unlock as the ball grows, a target with 1–3 stars, a results screen and a one-tap Roll again. This makes the 5-second, 1-minute and one-run loops of the design brief real. Design brief: README.md §2 (core loop, run flow), §3.3 (tiers and targets), §4 (HUD, results). Constitution: II, III, IV (runs with clear endings), VI, VII, IX. Depends on 001.

### Rules (src/shared/Rules, Lune-tested)
- Stars: 1 star at 20 studs, 2 at 45, 3 at 80 (proposed, in Config). Test the boundaries (19.99 → 0 stars, 20 → 1, 45 → 2, 80 → 3).
- Tier lookup: given a diameter and a world's tier table, return the current tier and the next tier's threshold.
- Coin reward: the sum of the `coins` value of every object absorbed in the run, then multipliers passed in as arguments (friend bonus, 2x Coins and Friend Boost pass arrive in 005 and 006; the order they apply in is defined here and tested). Coins are only ever added; knock-offs never take coins away (proposed; confirm in clarify).

### Content (src/shared/Content)
- A tier table per world as data: tier number, name key, diameter range and a "headline" object for the banner. For now, the grey-box room's 4 tiers; Snow Village's real tiers (README §3.3) come in 004 and must need no code change.

### Server: RunManager
- Each player has a personal run with a 4:00 timer (runDurationSeconds = 240 in Config). The server owns the timer and the result; the client only displays it.
- On spawn the run starts immediately. When the bell rings: the ball stops, and the server computes the final diameter, stars, coins earned, the object types collected for the first time this run, and the best final size among the player's Roblox friends in the server (`Player:IsFriendsWith`, cached).
- Roll again: resets the ball to start size at a spawn point and starts a new run in about 3 seconds, in the same server with the same friends. Back to hub: moves the player to a hub area in the same place (a simple grey-box plaza for now; the real hub art comes in 004).
- Edge cases: the player leaves mid-run (the run is dropped; nothing is granted), falls out of the world (respawn at their current size), or the server shuts down mid-run.
- Results are published once per run; any later persistence (003) must be able to apply them exactly once, so each run carries a unique run ID.

### Client: HUD and results (phone first)
- Top centre: timer (mm:ss) and the target size with star markers.
- Top left: size meter, the diameter as a large number plus a bar to the next tier, and "Next: [silhouette] CATS".
- Centre, short-lived: the pickup pop "+ Teddy Bear" with an icon (one line, fades in 1 s), and the tier banner "You can roll up: CATS!" while the camera pulls back.
- Feedback: every pickup gets a sound and a scale "pop", a light haptic pulse on mobile (`HapticService`), and camera shake on knock-offs. Grey-box placeholders for sounds and icons are fine here; final audio comes in 004.
- Results screen: final size, stars, coins, new book entries, the friend comparison, a large Roll again and a Back to hub. No discounts, no timers, nothing for sale here yet.
- Readability: text at least 18 px on phones, `UIScale` per viewport, buttons at least 96 px, a dark outline on every text element, and every status shown with an icon and a label (never colour or sound alone).

### Localisation (constitution IX, from day one)
Every player-facing string, including the tier banner and object names, comes from a LocalizationTable kept in the repo. English only in this feature; Spanish is completed in 007. No hard-coded strings in scripts or UI.

### Tests
- Lune: stars, tier lookup, coin reward with and without multipliers.
- Jest-Lua (Studio): the run ends on the server at 240 s even if the client stops sending anything; results are produced once per run ID; a client cannot end a run early or report a size.

### Acceptance
- A player can play three runs in a row with Roll again, each ending with a results screen.
- The HUD and results screen read correctly at a phone resolution (capture the viewport through the Studio MCP server).
- Tier banners appear when the diameter crosses a tier threshold, and only once per tier per run.
- No new console errors or warnings in a Studio playtest.

### Out of scope
Saving coins, best size or the book between sessions (003); party runs and merged balls (005); Shop (006); onboarding hints (007).

### Open questions (owner)
- Whether knock-offs can reduce the coins earned in a run (proposed: no).
- What the "friend comparison" shows when no friends are in the server (proposed: your own best size for this world, once 003 lands).
